SMSTunnel SMS

Odoo's texts from your own Android phone and your own number.

Choose Send via SMSTunnel next to Send via Odoo and Send via Twilio in Settings. From then on, every text Odoo sends for this company goes out from your own Android phone, with the SMSTunnel app: customers see a number they know and can reply to it, and each text costs what your carrier charges.

Needs an SMSTunnel account and the SMSTunnel app on an Android phone (the app is on Google Play; accounts and plans at smstunnel.io). Odoo sends each text and its number to SMSTunnel, which hands it to your phone. Privacy policy: smstunnel.io/privacy.

For Odoo 19.0 · Community and Enterprise

What it does

  • Works with everything in Odoo that sends texts: the SMS button on contacts and other records, SMS templates such as the delivery confirmation of Inventory, automated actions with "Send SMS", calendar and event reminders.
  • One text per number, sent from your own SIM.
  • Odoo's own statuses in the chatter: Sent, Processing while your phone is offline (the text waits and goes out when the phone is back), Delivered, or an error with SMSTunnel's reason.
  • Nothing is lost when SMSTunnel can't be reached: texts wait in Odoo's queue and go out later, automatically.
  • Per company: each company chooses Odoo, Twilio or SMSTunnel.
  • Check it from Settings: send a test text and list the phones of your account.
  • Texts people expect: order and delivery updates, reminders, replies. Texts of SMS Marketing mailings are refused.

Set it up in three steps

  1. Install the SMSTunnel app from Google Play on your Android phone and sign in. Sign in at dashboard.smstunnel.io with the same account: the phone appears under Phones.
  2. In the dashboard, open Apps → Add an app and copy the new key.
  3. In Odoo, open Settings → General Settings → Send SMS, choose Send via SMSTunnel, click Configure SMSTunnel, paste the key and send a test text to your own number.

Good to know

  • Where it runs: Odoo.sh and Odoo on your own server. Odoo Online does not install third-party apps.
  • Requirements: Odoo 19.0 with the core Twilio SMS module (sms_twilio, part of Odoo since September 2025), which Odoo installs together with this app. Nothing else to install on the server.
  • Numbers: in international format (+ and the country code) whenever possible; a default country code completes local numbers.
  • Limits: up to 1600 characters per text; SMSTunnel takes 30 texts a minute, and Odoo's queue sends the rest a little later.
  • The API key is stored on the company and only Settings administrators can read it. A copy of the database made by Odoo.sh for staging, or by odoo-bin neutralize, loses the key, so it never texts real customers.
  • For developers: texts created in code (sms.sms) go through SMSTunnel too; mark sign-in codes with smstunnel_sign_in_code to use the separate allowance for codes.
